🤯 Development Tips
===================

The tips in this sections are mostly small quirks that from time to time I forget about or specifics of how some things work.
So it's not really that useful. 
It's not even that much of "tips".

1. Guards applied to group commands also automatically get applied to its children commands, 
   e.g. if ``!7tv`` is a moderator-level command then ``!7tv add`` will also require moderator-level rights.
